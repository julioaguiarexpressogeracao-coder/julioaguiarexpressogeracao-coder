## About me

I'm Júlio Aguiar, an IT professional at Expresso Geração and a **Data Science student at the Federal University of Ceará (UFC)**.

My work spans **Data Analytics, Data Science, Statistics, and Business Intelligence (BI)**. I use **Python, SQL, Power BI, and Excel** to explore data, develop business metrics, and communicate findings through **data storytelling** to support decision-making.

I also work with **Artificial Intelligence, agent orchestration, prompt engineering, context engineering, AI workflows, and tool integration via the Model Context Protocol (MCP)**. I combine these skills with software development, automation, and system integration to address practical business problems, from analysis to implementation.

This professional account showcases part of that work: projects focused on logistics, resource management, and internal processes at Expresso Geração. You can find my learning projects and other initiatives on my main profile, **[@julioaguiar07](https://github.com/julioaguiar07)**.

## Areas of work and study

- **Data Analytics:** organizing, exploring, and interpreting data to identify patterns and answer business questions.
- **Data Science:** investigating problems and studying analytical methods and models to extract knowledge from data.
- **Statistics:** understanding variability, relationships, and uncertainty to support sound analysis and conclusions.
- **Business Intelligence:** building metrics, dashboards, and reports to monitor performance and inform decisions.
- **Data Storytelling:** turning analysis into clear narratives that connect context, visualizations, and findings for each audience.
- **Artificial Intelligence and agents:** designing AI workflows and orchestrating agents to carry out tasks.
- **Prompt and Context Engineering:** structuring instructions and relevant context to guide AI behavior and responses.
- **Tool Integration via MCP:** connecting AI applications and agents to tools and data sources through the Model Context Protocol.
- **Software Development and Automation:** building applications and integrations that connect data, people, and processes.
